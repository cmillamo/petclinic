# PC-303: show each pet's age on the owner's page

On the owner's page, each pet shows an "Age" entry directly after its birth date. The age is worked out when the page is shown, from the pet's birth date and today's date. Nothing new is stored.

## Scenarios

1. A pet one year old or older shows its age in whole years: "1 year", "3 years".
2. A pet under a year old shows its age in whole months: "1 month", "5 months".
3. A pet under a month old shows "under a month".
4. A pet with no stored birth date shows the Age label with an empty value.
5. A pet whose stored birth date is after today shows the Age label with an empty value. Such rows can exist: the edit form accepted future dates until that was fixed.
6. The label is "Age". The age texts are English in every language file for now.

## Out of scope

Storing the age, any schema change, and translations of the age texts.
